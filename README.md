# Athletic Footwear Pricing Analysis

## Overview
A competitive pricing analysis of 1,006 athletic footwear products across 10 major brands,
identifying market segmentation, brand positioning strategies, and underserved price points
in the running shoe category.

## Key Findings
- Adidas pursues near-exclusive premium positioning (80% of products above 121 USD),
  while Vans and Skechers compete almost entirely on value
- Performance categories (Running, CrossFit, Weightlifting) command a 2-3x price premium
  over lifestyle categories such as Skate and Walking
- A significant pricing gap exists at the 100-110 USD range in running shoes,
  representing an underserved market entry opportunity between budget and premium tiers

## Recommendation
For a new market entrant or repositioning brand, target the 95-115 USD range in running shoes,
differentiated by performance materials and positioned as an accessible alternative to
Asics and New Balance.
